# Recommendation after the visual input stopping rule

Date: 2026-10-01 (America/Chicago). **Proposed only; not approved or run.**
The [approved visual preparation](2026-10-01-visual-page-source-input-result.md)

## Remaining cause and concrete remedy

All supplied images are readable. Both independent contexts recognize three
of four diagnosed graphic omissions, but reject the fourth because its page
does not explicitly teach the requested conditional relation. One formerly
weak pair is Yes versus Unsure and another is Unsure in both. This is a
question/source relation and review-contract problem, not evidence that a
higher image resolution, larger model or another prompt sweep will fix it.

The draft no-match control has four unhelpful pages in both reviews, but its
question clarity was judged No/Unsure because the requested record was not
uniquely named. Clarification must be distinguished from failure to find
evidence: a question asking to identify an unknown entity may still specify
a clear category, relation and conditions. It should not need to contain the
answer or the identity it asks the learner to discover. A genuinely dangling
referent or incompatible conditions remain reasons to clarify.

Recommend one **bounded offline semantic/control reconciliation**, before
spending on another model trial:

1. Make that question-clarity distinction explicit in the prospective wire
   and measurement contract. Keep source-only, 0–3 unverified original-PDF
   links, one embedding and at most one future ID-only judgment. Do not
   restore answers or use outside facts to qualify a source.
2. Give one fresh blind adjudicator only the three unresolved semantic
   question/page pairs (P146, P119, P009), exact text/cues and their already
   frozen full-page images. Hide prior labels, reviewer votes and model
   choices. Separate a concrete on-page learning step from an assumed
   connection under an unstated condition. Record Yes/No/Unsure; do not
   demand a complete answer or force a positive judgment.
3. Have two fresh contexts independently check the draft four-page no-match
   control under the explicit clarity distinction. If it still lacks a
   clear target/category/relation, prepare one precise self-contained version
   on the same four pages and review it before admission. Preserve the
   unsuccessful draft/receipts as development evidence; admit at most one
   additional conclusive no-match group. All original 66 cases remain.
4. Preserve every historical label/result and current review freeze. Bind
   any prospective qualification/uncertainty overlay to the original tri-state
   mapping and full image inputs; never claim an old failed pilot passed.
   Unknown source judgments receive no usefulness credit and cannot become
   successful binary no-match. Keep the complete request/error denominator.
5. Determine whether the conservative available-input bound can satisfy the
   existing gates before proposing a paid trial. A complete input repair
   is not a requirement for 100% model accuracy; retain observed missing
   relations as misses. If a defensible label/control contract or the same
   quality gates remain impossible, stop and report that limitation.

## Boundaries

Only the same four cached public PDFs / already prepared images, at most
three existing semantic pairs plus one four-page control. No rerender,
download, model inference, private Knowledge, environment/key inspection,
database mutation, heldout opening, runtime/migration change or Ask activation.
Any local assembly/preflight stays within 4 CPU / 2 GiB / 600 seconds and
unchanged image/request caps. This is not training or a new model comparison.

Keep all original 66 calibration and 60 sealed heldout questions, difficult
cases and failed receipts. Keep >=90% all-displayed usefulness, hit/per-form,
exact-cardinality, no-match, availability, privacy/access and release gates.
The old heldout must receive a separately approved compatible independent
review before a future visual trial; do not open it in this reconciliation.
A later provider run still needs a fresh exact endpoint/model/price/calls/
image/token/time/cost envelope. Lane 6 stays **3/7**, Ask disabled.
