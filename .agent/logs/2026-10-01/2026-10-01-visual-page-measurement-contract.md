# Prospective public visual input measurement contract

Date: 2026-10-01 (America/Chicago). Prepared under the owner's
[offline visual-input approval](2026-10-01-visual-page-source-input-approved.md).
This preregistration precedes any visual model score. It grants no provider,
private-data, heldout or runtime execution.

## Preserved denominator and labels

Keep all 66 original exposed calibration questions and 264 candidate pairs,
with unchanged question/context/cue offsets and the completed audit's sealed
tri-state labels. Keep its 109 jointly useful page/cue labels, 152 joint weak
labels and three unresolved labels. Preserve every historical result and
ledger; do not rescore failed historical text-only trials as visual trials.

The conclusive positive strata remain 17 questions with one useful page,
21 with two, 14 with three and two with four. The latter must select exactly
three distinct useful IDs. Existing hit, per-form, exact-set/cardinality,
all-displayed usefulness, zero false no-match and failure-inclusive
availability fractions remain unchanged. Annotation is development evidence,
not evidence that a selector reaches these thresholds.

## Unknown source versus question clarification

The original unresolved question and its four source candidates remain.
`Unsure` is neither a weak label nor corpus-wide absence. An ID with either
page or cue `Unsure` cannot receive usefulness credit; if displayed it counts
in the displayed denominator without adding to the useful numerator. Its
selection is reported as an unresolved selection, never a verified source.

The prospective wire has a distinct `needs_clarification` question status.
It must have zero qualifying pages and zero selected IDs. Closed local parsing
enforces this without claiming that the entire corpus has no suitable source.
The independently reviewed unresolved-question control reports clarification
and zero selection separately. It is not added to the binary no-match count
or to conclusive exact-cardinality strata. The question remains in the total
request/availability denominator; errors remain errors, not safe abstentions.

The completed audit leaves 11 conclusive no-match groups. To preserve the
existing minimum of 12, retain the unresolved group and add at most one
independently reviewed four-page public control. It reuses four already cited
physical pages and asks a clear question about measured clinical-trial
survival results and compared treatments. Only two independent complete
reviews finding all four page/cue pairs unhelpful can admit that control.
Otherwise prospective trial readiness remains false; do not replace another
question or manufacture missing labels. This tests the supplied candidate
slate, not absence across the whole corpus.

## Actual-resolution input feasibility

Use the five diagnosed gaps and 16 prior pairs with independently agreed
weak page and cue labels. Choose the latter deterministically by SHA-256 of
`visual-feasibility-weak-v1:<pair ID>`, excluding the five gaps, before review.
Review packets omit old labels, gap/weak roles, model choices and cardinality.
Two fresh contexts judge readability, input usefulness and cue navigation at
the actual bounded full-page image resolution. Preserve `Unsure` and retain
all outcomes. These exposed cases are development controls, not a holdout or
a model accuracy measurement. Do not tune a second representation to their
labels under this approval.

Stop if the four diagnosed visual omissions remain unreadable/unrepresented,
if images turn agreed weak sources into apparently qualifying inputs, or if
the control/uncertainty contract remains unresolved. A disagreement requires
reporting the limitation; it does not automatically authorize more reviews,
another render resolution or another model trial.

## Wire, heldout and runtime boundaries

Freeze all 66 actual source/image/ID inputs and final assembled request
SHA/byte sizes. Bounds: four PNGs, 1,600-pixel long side, 2 MP and 1 MiB per
image, 6 MiB complete request, 4 CPU, 2 GiB job memory, 30 seconds per renderer
and 600 seconds total preparation. Images are faithful whole pages; no crop,
annotation, invented OCR or outside facts. The final verdict is closed issued
IDs/status/usefulness/cue flags, with 0–3 unverified links and no answers.

The 60 original heldout questions/labels remain unopened. Before any future
heldout trial, document and approve how independent blind source annotation
will align its rubric and visual-input representation while preserving all
original questions, uncertain cases, hard cases and release thresholds.
No original heldout label is presumed compatible just because calibration
input preparation succeeded.

Model quality, provider availability, image-token/cost guards, private-image
disclosure, a production renderer/dependency policy, migrations and Ask
enablement all retain separate gates. Byte bounds do not establish an input
token bound. No Gemini, token-count API, downloads, application-model
inference, real root environment, database or private Knowledge is used here.
