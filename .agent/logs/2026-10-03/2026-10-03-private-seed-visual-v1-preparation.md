## Implementation

New helpers:

- `scripts/prepare_private_seed_visual_trial_v1.py`: exact source/review/witness,
  request/profile, question/admission, PNG/cue/page/offset and runtime/code binding;
  supplied-response parsing and complete selected-card scoring.

  registry, transfer acknowledgements, stdin-only judge credential, container
  resource/deadline custody and default-inert CLI.

  fresh read-only/repeatable-read transactions, exact whole-wire render proof,
  current authorization/revision/profile/context and authenticated original-PDF
  rechecks after quota and after selection.
- `backend/scripts/rehearse_private_seed_visual_dispatch_v1.py`: current source
  binding and separately resource-fenced, provider-free SQL/PDF guard rehearsal.
- `backend/scripts/execute_private_seed_visual_trial_v1.py` and
  `backend/scripts/private_seed_visual_trial_entry_v1.py`: prospective one-use
  source-ID controller/entry; no default execution or credential lookup.
- `backend/tests/test_private_seed_visual_trial_v1.py`: invented, isolated
  fixtures only; no provider, operator configuration or retained data.

≥10/11 positive seed hits, a positive N12 hit, and empty clear U01/U02 slates.
Backend association never receives browser-rendering or release credit.
