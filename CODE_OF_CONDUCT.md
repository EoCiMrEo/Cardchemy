# Cardchemy Code of Conduct

Everyone should be able to participate safely, regardless of background,
identity, experience or ability. This policy applies to issues, pull requests,
discussions and other project spaces, and to people publicly representing
Cardchemy. It also covers private interactions connected to project work.

Treat people respectfully. Discuss the merits of a proposal, accept reasoned
disagreement, and acknowledge mistakes. Offer constructive, specific feedback
and give others room to learn. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual
advances, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and publication of another
person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Confidential reports and enforcement

Send conduct complaints privately to **eocimreo@gmail.com**. Include relevant
context and links where safe; do not publish a complaint or private evidence
in an issue. The project owner receives this mailbox. Maintainers restrict
report details to people needed for investigation and enforcement, and avoid
exposing the reporter's identity without permission unless legally required.
If a complaint involves a maintainer, identify that conflict so another
uninvolved reviewer can be sought where available. This small project cannot
promise an independent investigation body or a guaranteed response time.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation and explain
moderation decisions while protecting private information. Consequences depend
on severity and repetition: a corrective request or private warning, temporary
restrictions, or a permanent ban. Threats or serious harassment can lead to
immediate restriction. People subject to a decision may seek reconsideration
through the same private contact, providing additional context.

This project policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Security vulnerabilities use the separate confidential channel in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
